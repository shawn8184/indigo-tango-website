HR Ops

New-Hire Onboarding Desk

Get every new hire signed, set up, and ready without chasing anyone.

Who it's for

Restaurants, multi-location hourly employers, and 10 to 50 person companies that hire every month and still onboard by emailing PDFs.

The problem

Owners and office managers lose hours on every hire: chasing missing signatures, retyping the same details into payroll, and remembering to set up logins and access. In one owner's timed count it was 28 minutes of document data entry per hire, and 11 hires in a month. (source)

What we do

  • Map your current onboarding steps and find where handoffs break
  • Turn on and set up the onboarding features in the payroll or HR tools you already pay for, before adding anything new
  • Build one trigger from signed offer to a checklist with an owner and deadline for every step
  • Send new hires one link for signatures and uploads, with automatic reminders for anything missing
  • Move the new hire's details into payroll and your records once
  • Send IT and equipment setup tasks to the right person automatically
  • Keep a human review step for compliance forms

What you get

  • A working onboarding flow you can run for every hire
  • One status view showing who is complete and what is missing
  • A written runbook and a handoff session for your team

Next step

Book an assessment. We'll walk through your last few hires and show you where the time goes and what can run itself.
