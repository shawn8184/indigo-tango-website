WHO IT’S FOR

A growing company with tools already in place.

This work is a good fit if you run a growing business, you already have tools in place, and the problem is what happens between them.

  • Your inbox, spreadsheets, CRM, and accounting tools do not talk to each other.
  • The team is typing the same information into more than one system.
  • You have AI licenses, but they are not connected to the work people actually do.
  • Policies, processes, and tribal knowledge live in someone’s head, not somewhere the team can find.
  • You want permissions, approvals, and human exception handling in the workflow from day one.

WHO IT’S NOT FOR

Three kinds of work we don’t take.

  • Teams that want to rip out the software they already use and start over. We connect the systems you have already paid for and trained people on.
  • Organizations that only want a strategy deck. The point is to get operational with AI, not just get advised on it.
  • Anyone looking for a forced retainer after the first build. After a Build Sprint, you can keep working with us to optimize, or run the system internally.

04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Good questions. Straight answers.

Where should a growing company in Atlanta start with AI?

Start with the Assessment, not a tool purchase. We map where your team is losing time, what is worth automating, and what should stay human. You leave with a prioritized plan, a practical roadmap, and a fixed-price proposal.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. We connect the systems you already use — including Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, your CRM, ERP, accounting, and email — instead of ripping them out.

How long does an AI project take?

The Assessment takes about two weeks. Most first automations go from blueprint to production in four to eight weeks after that, depending on complexity. You get a realistic timeline at the end of the Assessment.

What do we have after a Build Sprint, and what happens next?

You have one production automation, agent, or integration, with permissions, a runbook, user acceptance testing, and 14-day hypercare. After that, we can keep tuning the system, train your team to run it, and identify the next bottleneck — or you can run it internally.

No. Indigo Tango is an AI consulting and automation firm for growing businesses, led by Shawn Wallis and focused especially in and around Atlanta. It is not Tango, the how-to or screenshot product used to capture standard operating procedures.
