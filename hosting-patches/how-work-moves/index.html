HOW WORK MOVES

The same job.
One context.

A private chat can make one person faster. The company changes when the request, the record, and the next person share one context.

We find the handoff worth fixing and build it on the systems you already run. A person still decides the exception.

01 / THREE LAYERS

Work already moves.
The record often does not.

01

People share the job.

An enquiry, a quote, an approval, a dispatch, or a customer update already passes between people. What is missing is one record of that pass.

02

AI prepares the handoff.

It classifies, drafts, and names the next owner. It shows what it used and what it does not know. Someone on your team reviews the exception.

03

Systems keep the record.

The update lands where the work already lives: the CRM, the job file, accounting, or the inbox. Writing rights match the person who would have typed it.

02 / THE ASSESSMENT

Four questions before anything is built.

  1. 01

    Which handoff is worth fixing?

    We look for the repeat pass that costs the team time, not a tour of every tool.

  2. 02

    Who has to decide?

    The person who owns the exception stays in the path. AI prepares the work. It does not close the case alone.

  3. 03

    Where does the record already live?

    We use the inbox, CRM, accounting, or job file you have already trained people on.

  4. 04

    What does done look like?

    Fewer retypes. A clearer next step. A result your team can run after the build.

03 / THE PATH

Start with the handoff.

The assessment takes about two weeks and ends in a fixed-price proposal. A build sprint is four to eight weeks, after the scope is agreed. After that, your team can run it.
