AI Consulting Questions, Answered

What does an engagement with you actually look like?

We figure out how your business actually works, not how the org chart says it works. Then we prioritize, build, and stick around instead of vanishing after the invoice clears.

Where do we even start?

The Assessment. It's not optional, it's not a sales trick, and it's the only way to know what's actually worth fixing before we build anything.

Do we have to replace our existing systems?

No. We're not in the business of ripping out your CRM so we can sell you a new one. We connect what you already run — Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, your CRM — and make it behave.

How is the AI Opportunity Assessment scoped?

Fixed scope, about two weeks. You leave with a prioritized blueprint and a Build Sprint proposal — quoted after diagnosis, not a 40-page strategy novel.

What comes after the Assessment?

A Build Sprint — one production system with runbook, UAT, and 14-day hypercare. Then an Optimize Retainer if you want ongoing adoption and the next bottleneck fixed.

How do you keep AI adoption controlled?

Governance isn't an afterthought we bolt on after something breaks. Permissions, approvals, and audit trails are built in from day one, because “we'll secure it later” is how companies end up in the news.

How do you measure whether this is actually working?

Hours saved, errors reduced, faster response times, more capacity without hiring. If we can't point to a number, we didn't do our job.

Do you disappear after the first project?

No. Ongoing consulting means we keep tuning, expanding, and fixing the next bottleneck. We don't do drive-by consulting.
