BEFORE WE START

Clear scope. Clear next steps.

First, a free conversation.

A short introductory conversation helps us check fit and recommend a package. No paid assessment is required for AI Kickstart or either Second Brain package.

AI Kickstart is paid upfront. Second Brain packages are 50% upfront and 50% at handover. We confirm the written scope and timing before payment; this website does not take payment.

Subscriptions, document cleanup or OCR, live connectors, custom code, and automated actions are excluded from the setup packages. Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted before work begins.
