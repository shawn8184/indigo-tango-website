06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Something else on your mind?

Let’s talk ↗

Where should our company start with AI?

Start with the Assessment, not a tool purchase. We map where your team is losing time, what is worth automating, and what should stay human. You leave with a prioritized plan.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. We connect the systems you already use — your CRM, ERP, accounting, and email — instead of ripping them out. AI Operations works with the tools you have already paid for and trained your team on.

How long does an AI project take?

The Assessment takes about two weeks. Most first automations go from blueprint to production in four to eight weeks after that, depending on complexity. You get a realistic timeline at the end of the Assessment.

What happens after implementation?

We can keep tuning the automation, train your team to run it, and identify the next bottleneck worth fixing. You can also choose to run it internally — that choice is yours, not a forced retainer.
