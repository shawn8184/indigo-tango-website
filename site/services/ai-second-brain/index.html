A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what
you’re buying.

Is this just a set of prompts?

You receive a configured workspace, a curated source pack, instructions, three tested task workflows, and a handover guide. Prompts are one part of the setup.

Do I need both ChatGPT and Claude?

No. We choose one primary platform around your tasks, approved account, and access needs. Features and sharing depend on your plan and workspace settings.

Will it remember everything or learn automatically?

No setup guarantees complete recall or perfect answers. We configure the available context and memory settings, test representative tasks, and establish a deliberate review and update routine.

How much does it cost?

AI Second Brain Setup is $1,950 once; Second Brain for Teams is $2,950 once. Guided AI Kickstart help is $495. Optional Workspace Care is $295 per month. Software subscriptions are separate. No paid assessment is required for these packages; we confirm fit and written scope before payment.

Can it connect to our company systems?

Connections may be possible depending on the platform and your access policies. Live connectors, ongoing sync, and automated actions require a separate technical scope and approval.
