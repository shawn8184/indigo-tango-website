05 / A FEW DETAILS

Clear expectations.
From the start.

Where should we start?

Start with the AI Opportunity Assessment. We map your operations, identify what is worth automating, and provide a prioritized roadmap before choosing tools or building a system.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. We connect the systems you already use, including Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, and your CRM. The goal is better operations with a practical fit for your business.

What will we have after a Build Sprint?

One production automation, agent, or integration, with permissions, a runbook, user acceptance testing, and 14-day hypercare. Scope and pricing are agreed before the build.

How do you measure success?

We tie the work to outcomes such as hours saved, fewer errors, faster response times, and more capacity. The assessment helps establish which measures matter for your business.
